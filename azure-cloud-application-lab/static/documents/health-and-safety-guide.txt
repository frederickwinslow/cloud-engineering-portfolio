HEALTH AND SAFETY GUIDE (SAMPLE)

Purpose
This sample guide reminds readers to follow site-specific safety procedures. It is not a substitute for formal training or emergency instructions.

Everyday safety
Keep walkways clear, use equipment only as trained, and report hazards or near misses promptly.

Emergency
Follow posted evacuation instructions and local emergency procedures. Contact emergency services when needed.

Reporting
Notify your supervisor or designated safety contact about injuries, hazards, and unsafe conditions as soon as possible.

Review note
Replace this sample with approved, location-specific safety guidance before using the portal for business.
